// case study

How DANI works

01

Problem

Most AI assistants do one thing. I wanted one that codes, chats naturally, understands screens, creates images, and actually speaks.

02

What I built

An AI assistant powered by my own models, fine-tuned from scratch — no third-party AI APIs. Every capability, from conversation to vision to image generation, is being trained by me, end to end.

03

Technologies

Node.js, TypeScript, Python, custom model training & fine-tuning pipelines, text-to-speech, WhatsApp automation, REST APIs.

04

Features

Chat · coding assistance · vision (screenshots & screen recording) · realistic image generation & editing · voice replies · sticker memory · file handling.

05

Result

An assistant built entirely on my own models — currently in training and development, with a live waitlist collecting early users ahead of public launch.
